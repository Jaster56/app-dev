# The Spy Next Door

> *"The world's greatest spy has a new mission: babysitting."*

---

## Introduction

**The Spy Next Door** is a funny action movie starring Jackie Chan. It is also known as *Kung Fu Nanny* in some countries. The story mixes exciting fights with silly family moments, and it is made for kids and families to enjoy together.

- **Genre:** Action, Comedy, Family
- **Release Year:** 2010
- **Main Actor:** Jackie Chan

## Synopsis

Bob Ho is a secret agent who works for the CIA, but his neighbors think he is just a boring pen salesman. Bob falls in love with his neighbor Gillian, and he wants to be a good partner to her. Gillian has to leave town for a while, so Bob agrees to watch her three kids. The kids do not trust him at first, and they make his job very hard. One of the kids, Ian, accidentally downloads a secret code from Bob's computer. Bad guys want that code, so they start chasing the family. Bob has to use his kung fu skills to keep the kids safe. In the end, the kids learn to love Bob, and he learns that family is more important than missions.

## Characters

1. **Bob Ho** - A kind and funny spy who pretends to be a pen salesman.
2. **Gillian** - Bob's neighbor and girlfriend, who is a loving mother.
3. **Farren** - Gillian's oldest child, who is a teenager and does not trust Bob at first.
4. **Ian** - Gillian's son, who accidentally downloads the secret code.
5. **Nora** - Gillian's youngest child, who is sweet and cute.
6. **Anton Poldark** - The main villain, who wants to steal the secret code.
